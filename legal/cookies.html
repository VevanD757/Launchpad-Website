Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. This page says which ones Launchpad uses.

What this site sets

Launchpad uses limited analytics and advertising measurement.

  • Strictly necessary: a signed cookie for the screening dashboard, which only staff use. The public site and application form still work when optional tracking is blocked.
  • Analytics: Vercel Analytics counts page visits. It does not use cookies to identify you across sites.
  • Advertising measurement: when enabled, the Meta Pixel records page views and explicit Launchpad funnel actions, such as starting or submitting an application and following a link to Stanley. It does not receive application answers. Meta may use cookies and identifiers supplied with an ad click to measure whether an ad led to those actions.
  • Your saved answers: the application keeps your answers in your browser's local storage so you do not lose them on a refresh. That never leaves your device until you submit, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Third-party content

If you reach the interview stage, the booking link opens a page on stan.store. That page is covered by Stan's general Privacy Policy and cookie notice, not this one.

Turning them off

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking optional analytics or advertising measurement will not stop you using the site or applying. Blocking the strictly necessary staff cookie will sign you out of the screening dashboard if you are staff.
